About me

Curious about data, uncertainty, and reliability.

I am a research scholar at IIT Patna working in statistical inference and reliability theory. My research interests include developing and studying statistical methods for lifetime data, particularly when observations are censored or arise from dependent failure mechanisms.

01

Research approach

I am interested in mathematical formulation, likelihood-based inference, estimator properties, simulation studies, and applying statistical methods to reliability problems.

02

What I explore

My work spans lifetime distributions, censoring schemes, competing-risks models, accelerated life testing, and process capability or acceptance-sampling problems.

Statistical inferenceReliability analysisSurvival analysisLifetime dataR programmingClassical and Bayesian estimation
Research interests

Questions I am working on

These themes reflect my research interests and ongoing technical explorations. Update each description to match the exact scope of your thesis and current projects.

RESEARCH THEME 01

Competing risks and dependence

Inference for dependent competing-risks models, copula-based dependence, and lifetime data observed under progressive censoring schemes.

Copulas · Competing risks · Censoring
RESEARCH THEME 02

Reliability and accelerated life testing

Reliability assessment and test-design questions for lifetime models, including stress-based experiments and estimation of reliability at normal operating conditions.

Reliability · ALT · Optimal design
RESEARCH THEME 03

Acceptance sampling and process capability

Sampling-plan construction using process capability indices, operating-characteristic constraints, censoring, and inspection or warranty-related costs.

Acceptance sampling · Cpy · Cost design
RESEARCH THEME 04

Lifetime distributions

Statistical estimation and model assessment for lifetime distributions, including Chen-type models and related reliability applications.

MLE · Estimation · Goodness of fit
RESEARCH THEME 05

Inference under censoring

Exploring likelihood construction and estimation methods under Type-II, hybrid, and progressive censoring schemes.

Likelihood · Censoring · Simulation
RESEARCH THEME 06

Computational statistics

Using computational experiments to examine estimator performance, compare methods, and study statistical procedures across parameter settings.

R · Numerical methods · Simulation
